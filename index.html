HOW TO PLAY

HEXATAFL

Hexatafl is a Viking-inspired strategy game based on the ancient game of Hnefatafl.

THE GOAL

Red — The Defenders
Protect the King and move him to any of the six Escape Hexes around the edge of the board.

Black — The Attackers
Surround and capture the King before he can escape.

WHO MOVES FIRST?

Black always moves first.

HOW PIECES MOVE

All pieces can move any number of empty hexes in a straight line along any of the six directions.

Pieces cannot jump over other pieces.

CAPTURING

A normal piece is captured when it is trapped between two enemy pieces on opposite sides along a straight line.

Only the player making the move can capture. A piece is not captured simply because it moves between two enemy pieces.

THE KING

The King is the most important piece on the board.

Normally, the King is captured when attackers occupy three of the hexes surrounding him.

If the King is in a corner with only three neighboring hexes, two attackers can capture him from opposite sides of the corner.

THE THRONE

The gray hex in the center of the board is the Throne.

Only the King may occupy the Throne.

Other pieces may pass through the Throne when it is empty, but they cannot stop there.

THE ESCAPE HEXES

The six gray hexes around the edge of the board are Escape Hexes.

Only the King may enter an Escape Hex.

The King immediately wins the game by reaching any Escape Hex.

MANDATORY ESCAPE

If the King ends his turn next to an Escape Hex and can legally move onto it on his next turn, he must escape or risk being captured.

If the King chooses not to escape, an attacker directly opposite the Escape Hex can capture him.

STALEMATE

When all players have no viable moves left.

HOW TO WIN

RED WINS when the King reaches an Escape Hex.

BLACK WINS when the King is captured.

A player with no legal moves also loses.

Select a piece to see its legal moves.
